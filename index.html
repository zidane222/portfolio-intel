How this is calculated

Starts with $10,000 on the member's first filing date in the past year. On each filing date, stocks they bought are added and stocks they sold are dropped, then the holdings are split equally. Trades are acted on the day they were filed, not the day she traded, since that is when the public could see them. Bonds and private assets are skipped. Options are treated as the underlying stock. No fees, taxes, or dividends. Past results don't predict future ones; not investment advice.

Risk.

How much each investment swings, from cash to single stocks.

How risk is measured

Volatility is how much the price swings in a typical year, from the last 12 months of daily prices. Worst drop is the biggest fall from a peak in that year. Market sensitivity (beta) is how much it tends to move when the S&P 500 moves 1%. Levels: Very low under 1% volatility (cash, T-bills), Low under 8% (most bond funds), Moderate under 20% (broad stock funds like the S&P 500), High under 35%, Very high above that (many single stocks). Past swings don't guarantee future ones.

Get alerts.

A push notification when a new filing hits your stocks or the members you follow.

  1. Install ntfy, a free notification app. App Store · Google Play
  2. Subscribe to your private topic in the app:
  3. Tell the daily updater what to watch. In your GitHub repo, open Settings · Secrets and variables · Actions · Variables and add:
    NTFY_TOPIC
    ALERT_TICKERS
    ALERT_MEMBERSPelosi
    ALERT_SECTORS(optional)
    ALERT_TYPE(optional)
    ALERT_MIN_AMOUNT(optional)

You get a notification when a new filing matches your tickers or members. If you also set industry, type or minimum size, those must match too. Use Alert me on this in Latest disclosures to fill these in from your filters. Tickers come from your portfolio and watchlist. The check runs once a day with the filings update.
